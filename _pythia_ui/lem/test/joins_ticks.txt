# Ticks of joins.lem, on lem alone. Each case is a tick on the facts that
# the cases before it left: `=== title`; lines that assert (`+ Fact`) or
# retract (`- Fact`) the host's facts, or show a relation's facts after the
# tick (`? Name`); `---`; then the facts asked for.

=== a triangle, and three edges with no way back
+ E(1, 2)
+ E(2, 3)
+ E(1, 3)
? Tri
? OneWay
---
Tri(1, 2, 3)
OneWay(1, 2)
OneWay(1, 3)
OneWay(2, 3)
