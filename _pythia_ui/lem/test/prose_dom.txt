=== paragraphs: emphasis, strong, struck, code, a word with underscores, and a line's break
["+", "Text", {"n": 1, "text": "A *word*, a **strong** one, ~~gone~~, `a ``tick`` here`, snake_case_name, and\na break:  \nhere.\n\nAnother paragraph, ending\\\nin a backslash's break."}]
---

main.g-window
  div.g-prose
    p
      "A "
      em "word"
      ", a "
      strong "strong"
      " one, "
      del "gone"
      ", "
      code.g-prose-code "a ``tick`` here"
      ", snake_case_name, and\na break:"
      br
      "here."
    p
      "Another paragraph, ending"
      br
      "in a backslash's break."

=== fenced code, as written, and a diff's lines as a diff's
["+", "Text", {"n": 1, "text": "```js\nvar a = 1 < 2;\n  indented\n```\n~~~diff\n+one\n-two\n~~~"}]
---
page title: "prose"
main.g-window
  div.g-prose
    pre.g-code "var a = 1 < 2;\n  indented"
    pre.g-code.g-diff
      span.g-diff-add "+one\n"
      span.g-diff-del "-two"

        tr

          td.g-align-center

=== links: the web's in a new tab, without a referrer; the host's own; any other as text
["+", "Text", {"n": 1, "text": "[docs](https://example.com/a_b) <https://x.org> see https://y.org/p(q). [own](/c/1) [bad](javascript:alert(1)) ![pic](https://example.com/p.png)"}]

      " "
      a.g-prose-link href="https://example.com/p.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" "pic"
