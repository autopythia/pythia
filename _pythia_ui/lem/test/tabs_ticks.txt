# Ticks of tabs.lem, after glem's prelude, glui's files and a theme: the tab
# strip of the glui view of pythia's node server. Each case is a tick that
# runs on the facts that the cases before it left: `=== title`; lines that
# assert (`+ Fact`) or retract (`- Fact`) the host's facts, send a message
# (`! Msg`), or show a relation's facts after the tick (`? Name`); `---`;
# then the widget tree, the facts asked for, and the effects sent.

=== the index alone: its tab shows, and can't be closed
+ Open(0, "Root contexts", 0)
+ Shown(0)
---
Hud(Strip, place=TopLeft)
  Tabs(Strips)
    TabItem(TabOf(0), text="Root contexts", active=True, closes=False)

=== a context opens, after the index, and shows
+ Open(10, "ui-0 (10)", 1)
+ Shown(10)
---
Hud(Strip, place=TopLeft)
  Tabs(Strips)
    TabItem(TabOf(0), text="Root contexts", active=False, closes=False)
    TabItem(TabOf(10), text="ui-0 (10)", active=True, closes=True)

=== another opens, behind it
+ Open(3, "wui-1 (3)", 2)
---
Hud(Strip, place=TopLeft)
  Tabs(Strips)
    TabItem(TabOf(0), text="Root contexts", active=False, closes=False)
    TabItem(TabOf(10), text="ui-0 (10)", active=True, closes=True)
    TabItem(TabOf(3), text="wui-1 (3)", active=False, closes=True)

=== picking the index asks the host to show it
! Chosen(TabOf(0))
---
Hud(Strip, place=TopLeft)
  Tabs(Strips)
    TabItem(TabOf(0), text="Root contexts", active=False, closes=False)
    TabItem(TabOf(10), text="ui-0 (10)", active=True, closes=True)
    TabItem(TabOf(3), text="wui-1 (3)", active=False, closes=True)
effect Show(0)

=== closing a context's tab asks the host to close it
! Dismissed(TabOf(10))
---
Hud(Strip, place=TopLeft)
  Tabs(Strips)
    TabItem(TabOf(0), text="Root contexts", active=False, closes=False)
    TabItem(TabOf(10), text="ui-0 (10)", active=True, closes=True)
    TabItem(TabOf(3), text="wui-1 (3)", active=False, closes=True)
effect Close(10)

=== the host closes it, and shows the next
- Open(10, "ui-0 (10)", 1)
+ Shown(3)
---
Hud(Strip, place=TopLeft)
  Tabs(Strips)
    TabItem(TabOf(0), text="Root contexts", active=False, closes=False)
    TabItem(TabOf(3), text="wui-1 (3)", active=True, closes=True)
