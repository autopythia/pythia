# Ticks of contexts.lem, after glem's prelude, glui's files and a theme.
# Each case is a tick that runs on the facts that the cases before it
# left: `=== title`; lines that assert (`+ Fact`) or retract (`- Fact`) the
# host's facts, send a message (`! Msg`), or show a relation's facts after
# the tick (`? Name`); `---`; then the widget tree, the facts asked for,
# and the effects sent.
